# FreeCoffee.bio

The 100% free, open-source & self-hosted tipping and link-in-bio platform for creators. 0% platform fees.

## 🚀 Key Features

- **0% Platform Fees**: Keep 100% of your earnings with direct payment gateways.
- **Link-in-Bio + Tipping**: Combine your bio links and donation engine in one place.
- **Self-Hosted & Privacy First**: Deploy on your own infrastructure with total control.
- **Open Source**: Built for creators and developers worldwide.
